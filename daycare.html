Day-Care ENT Surgery in Thrissur

What is day-care surgery?

Day-care (ambulatory) surgery means you go home a few hours after your operation, on the same day. It can be done under local, regional or general anaesthesia.

Why choose day-care surgery?

  • Newer techniques allow most ENT operations to be done as day care.
  • It reduces the cost of treatment.
  • Insurance companies accept day-care surgery.
  • You recover in the comfort of your own home.

Which ENT operations can be done as day care?

  • Coblation adenoid surgery
  • Coblation intracapsular tonsil surgery
  • Nasal septum surgery and endoscopic sinus and polyp surgery
  • Endoscopic ear surgery to close a hole in the eardrum
  • Coblation snoring surgery
  • Surgery for vocal nodules or polyps
  • Procedures under local anaesthesia

Who may need to stay in hospital?

  • People with uncontrolled high blood pressure, diabetes, or heart or lung disease
  • Children under 3 years and adults over 65
  • People living more than an hour by road from the hospital

Your surgeon and anaesthetist will advise what is safest for you.
