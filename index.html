Why patients choose MIDAS ENT

  • Home the same day. Most operations are done as day-care surgery at MIDAS Hospital, Ollur.
  • Surgery only when it's the better option for you. We try medicines and simple treatments first whenever they can work. When surgery is needed, we plan the operation that best suits your problem and your age.
  • Advanced, tissue-friendly techniques. Endoscopic surgery, even for the ear and the salivary glands, and the latest-generation coblation system, which allows function-preserving adenoid and tonsil surgery. MIDAS Hospital was among the first in Thrissur to offer latest-generation coblation and sialendoscopy (2024), and balloon Eustachian tube and balloon sinus surgery (2025).
  • One team, written protocols. Every MIDAS ENT doctor follows the same written protocols, so the standard of care is the same whoever you see. Meet our doctors.
  • Sleep apnoea surgery planned on evidence. Sleep endoscopy (DISE) shows exactly where your airway closes before any operation is planned.
  • Your child's privacy. We never ask you to send your child's sleep video by WhatsApp. Show it to us in the clinic.

What to expect at your visit

  • We listen and examine you, often with an endoscope, a thin camera, to see inside the nose, throat or ear.
  • Many problems are treated with medicines or a procedure in the clinic, such as wax removal or a vertigo correction manoeuvre.
  • If tests are needed, such as a hearing test, scan or sleep study, we explain why before arranging them.
  • When surgery is needed, most is done as day-care surgery.
