About MIDAS ENT

MIDAS stands for Minimally Invasive Daycare Advanced Surgery. It began at MIDAS Hospital and Surgical Centre, Ollur, a hospital built for day-care surgery, where most operations are done with modern, minimally invasive techniques and patients go home the same day.

Our approach: MERA

Maximising Effectiveness, Retaining Anatomy. Every treatment is planned around two questions: what needs to be treated, and what should be protected. Diseased tissue is treated fully. Healthy tissue that still works is kept.

For example, the amount of adenoid or tonsil removed in a child is graded to the child's age and problem, rather than everything being removed by default.

We use advanced, tissue-friendly techniques: endoscopic surgery, even for the ear and the salivary glands, and the Werewolf Coblation system for function-preserving adenoid and tonsil surgery. MIDAS Hospital introduced Werewolf Coblation and sialendoscopy in Thrissur in 2024.

One team, one standard

MIDAS ENT is a team of ENT surgeons and visiting specialists, led by Dr Rejith R S. Every doctor works from the same written MIDAS ENT protocols for diagnosis, treatment and surgery, so you get the same standard of care whichever doctor you see and whichever MIDAS ENT clinic you visit. Growing across Thrissur

MIDAS ENT runs ENT clinics and departments in partner hospitals, so care is closer to home:

  • MIDAS ENT at MIDAS Hospital, Ollur: our main centre for day-care surgery
  • MIDAS ENT at Metropolitan Hospital, Thrissur: evening clinic

Wherever you are seen, you have the same team and the same protocols. Surgery can be done at MIDAS Hospital or at the partner hospital, with the senior surgeons. New clinics will be listed here as they open. See clinics and timings.

Training ENT surgeons

MIDAS ENT trains young ENT surgeons in the MERA approach and the MIDAS ENT protocols, so that the same careful, function-preserving care can reach more patients.

Free self-checks

Our free self-checks, such as the MERA Sleep Apnoea Check and the Hearing Check, are built from the same protocols. They tell you how soon you should be seen.
