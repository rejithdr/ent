Hearing Check

For adults and children with hearing loss in one or both ears. For sudden hearing loss, a simple hum test you do at home helps show how urgently you need to be seen.

This is a check, not a diagnosis, and it gives no treatment advice. Your answers stay on your phone or computer and are not stored or sent anywhere.
Sudden hearing loss is an emergency. The chance of recovery is best when treatment starts within 72 hours.
