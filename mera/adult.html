MERA Sleep Apnoea Check

A self-check for adults (18 years and above) who snore or feel sleepy during the day. Questions come one at a time, and the check asks only what your answers need.

This is a self-check, not a diagnosis. Your answers stay on your phone or computer and are not stored or sent anywhere.

For children under 18, use the MERA Child Sleep Apnoea Check.
