Our approach: MIDAS MERA

Maximising Effectiveness, Retaining Anatomy. Every operation is planned around what needs to be treated and what should be protected. Where tissue is diseased, it is treated fully. Where it still works, we try to keep it.

For children's adenoid and tonsil surgery, this means a defined, graded protocol: the amount of tissue removed is planned according to the child's age and the problem being treated.
