MERA Child Sleep Apnoea Score

A check for parents of children aged 2 to 17 who snore or breathe through the mouth. Answer each question, and you'll see how likely obstructive sleep apnoea is.

This is a check, not a diagnosis. Your answers stay on your phone or computer and are not stored or sent anywhere. For 18 years and above, use the adult MERA Sleep Apnoea Score. For a child under 2 who snores, please see a doctor directly.
