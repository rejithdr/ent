MERA sleep apnoea checks

MERA Child Sleep Apnoea Check

A check for parents of children aged 2 to 17 who snore, breathe through the mouth, or have frequent colds or ear problems. You'll get a short report on the adenoids, any complications, and tonsil size.

This is a self-check, not a diagnosis. Your answers stay on your phone or computer and are not stored or sent anywhere.

For 18 years and above, use the MERA Sleep Apnoea Check. For a child under 2 who snores, please see a doctor.

Growth is checked against the WHO Child Growth Standards (2006) up to 5 years and the WHO Growth Reference (2007) from 5 to 19 years.
